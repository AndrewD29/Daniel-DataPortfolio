Customer Care Voucher Analysis

Situation

Following an outsourcing initiative, customer care voucher expenditure increased significantly. Stakeholders wanted to understand the cause and identify improvement opportunities.

Task

Analyse historical data and identify whether changes in operational processes were contributing to increased costs.

Action

Extracted 36 months of voucher data using SQL, joined employee records and operational data sources, cleansed the dataset, created trend analysis and presented findings to stakeholders.

Result

The analysis identified a process and training issue linked to the outsourcing programme. Recommendations were implemented, helping reduce unnecessary expenditure and improve operational consistency.
